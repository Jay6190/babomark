Compliance Standard: Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act

1. Introduction

At Babomark Limited, accessible from our corporate website, protecting the privacy and security of our clients, sub-contractors, partners, and site visitors is of utmost importance. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal data we collect, how we use and safeguard it, and your rights in accordance with the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act and international data privacy regulations.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, request a tender quotation, or submit project inquiries, we may collect personal information including but not limited to:

  • Contact Information: Full name, corporate email address, telephone numbers, and physical/postal address.
  • Project & Tender Inquiries: Specifications, site locations, engineering documentation, and company details provided via our service request forms.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and website usage statistics collected via cookies for operational improvement.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected personal information strictly for legitimate commercial and operational purposes, including:

  • Processing and responding to inquiries regarding building construction, telecom infrastructure, roads, and agribusiness engineering.
  • Preparing accurate tender documents, structural estimates, and commercial project contracts.
  • Communicating updates, schedule milestones, and HSE safety advisories for ongoing site operations.
  • Ensuring system security, preventing unauthorized activity, and maintaining organizational compliance.

4. Data Sharing and Protection

Babomark Limited does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies. We only share information with trusted third parties under strict confidentiality under the following circumstances:

  • Regulatory & Legal Requirements: Where required by Tanzanian statutory authorities or regulatory compliance laws.
  • Authorized Partners: Sub-contractors or technical consultants who strictly require project details to perform specific engineering duties on our behalf.

We implement robust technical, physical, and administrative measures—including encrypted server communications and strict access controls—to safeguard your data against loss, misuse, or unauthorized access.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by statutory record-keeping laws. You hold the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you or your organization.
  • Request corrections or updates to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request the deletion of your personal data where retention is no longer legally mandated.
